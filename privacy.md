# Elevated Emojis Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 30, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 30, 2026

This page is the Privacy Policy for **Elevated Emojis** (bundle ID `com.ketchupdrool.ElevatedEmojis`), published by Ketchup Drool / Shawn Wright.

For a stable deep link used in App Store Connect and the in-app paywall, use: [Privacy Policy](./privacy).

## The Short Version

- **No accounts.** Elevated Emojis does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
- **Local data only.** Favorites, recent stickers, premium entitlement status, and any stickers you import stay on your iPhone (including an App Group shared with the keyboard extension).
- **Purchases go through Apple.** Premium subscriptions use StoreKit / In-App Purchase. We do not receive your payment card number.
- **No ads.** The app does not show ads and does not sell your information.

## 1. Who We Are

## 2. Information the App Stores on Your Device

Elevated Emojis does not run a user account service. On your device it may store:

- **Favorites and recently used sticker IDs** in the App Group container (`group.com.ketchupdrool.ElevatedEmojis`) so the host app and keyboard can share them.
- **Imported sticker packs** you add yourself, as PNG files in that App Group container.
- **Premium entitlement status** derived from Apple’s StoreKit transactions (so the keyboard can unlock paid categories without selling subscriptions itself).
- **Appearance / settings choices** that stay on the device.

Bundled starter stickers ship inside the app binary. They are not uploaded from your phone by this app.

## 3. Purchases and Apple

Optional **Elevated Emojis Premium** (monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription) is sold through Apple’s In-App Purchase. Apple processes payment. Elevated Emojis receives subscription status / transaction identifiers needed to unlock premium sticker categories and to restore purchases. It does not receive your full payment card, billing address, or Apple ID password.

Apple’s privacy practices apply to App Store billing: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.

Subscription terms are governed by [Apple’s Licensed Application End User License Agreement (Standard EULA)](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/). See also our [Terms of Use](./terms).

## 4. What Elevated Emojis Does Not Do

- Does not require an account or collect an email address inside the app.
- Does not show advertisements in the app.
- Does not sell, rent, or share personal information for advertising.
- Does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the current product.
- Does not send your favorites, imported stickers, or keyboard contents to our servers (there is no Elevated Emojis account backend).

Full Access for the keyboard is an iOS setting you control. Enable it only if you need features that require pasteboard / App Group access as described in the app’s setup screens.

## 5. Children

Elevated Emojis is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
